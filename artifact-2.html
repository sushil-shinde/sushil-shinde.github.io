Artifact 2: Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning

View the Full ML vs. Deep Learning Report

Course: AIML-500 Machine Learning Fundamentals

Project Type: Research and Comparative Analysis

Introduction

This artifact is a report I developed to compare traditional machine learning and deep learning using real-world applications. The project helped me understand how different AI approaches solve problems and why selecting the right technology matters.

Description

I examined two applications:

  • Apache SpamAssassin: An email filtering system that uses traditional machine learning techniques, including Bayesian classification.
  • LumineticsCore: A medical screening system that uses deep learning techniques to analyze retinal images for signs of diabetic eye disease.

I compared how these approaches work, their benefits, and their limitations.

Objective

My objective was to understand when traditional machine learning is more practical and when deep learning provides an advantage. I also wanted to learn how data characteristics, model complexity, and computing requirements influence technology decisions.

Process

I started by reviewing the differences between machine learning and deep learning in our course lesson. Next, I researched two real-world applications, examining how each system uses data and why its selected approach fits the problem. I also considered why the alternative approach might be less practical. Finally, I prepared a written report with a comparison table, explanations, and supporting references.

Artifact-Specific Value Proposition

This artifact demonstrates my ability to evaluate different AI technologies and explain their practical benefits and limitations. For technology teams and employers, it shows how I approach technical decisions by considering the problem, available data, complexity, and system requirements.

Unique Value

The report compares two applications from different industries. It demonstrates that the most advanced technology is not always the best choice. A simpler model may be more practical for some problems, while deep learning may be better suited for complex image analysis.

Relevance to My Professional Goals

My experience in financial technology has shown me the importance of selecting reliable and efficient technical solutions. As I continue learning AI and machine learning, I want to develop the ability to evaluate models based on real business needs rather than technology trends.

Outcomes and Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of traditional machine learning and deep learning. I learned that model selection depends on the problem, data, complexity, and resources available. It also helped me improve my ability to explain technical concepts to a professional audience.
