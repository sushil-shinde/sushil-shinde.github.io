Artifact 4: Leading Responsible AI in Financial Technology

A Practical Framework for Recognizing and Reducing Human Bias

Includes a worked example showing how the checklist changed a rollout decision from GO to HOLD when subgroup false-alert rates differed by 3×.

Introduction

This artifact presents a practical framework for recognizing and reducing human bias in AI systems. It focuses on responsible AI development, fairness evaluation, human oversight, and leadership decision-making.

Using financial transaction monitoring as an example, the framework shows how leaders can identify risks, question assumptions, and evaluate evidence before introducing or expanding AI systems.

Objective

The objective of this artifact is to demonstrate how leaders can recognize human bias, evaluate AI risks, and support responsible AI adoption in financial technology.

Process

I began by examining five ways human bias can influence AI systems: data selection, labeling, algorithm design, testing, and interpretation of results.

I then connected these concepts to financial technology, particularly AI-assisted transaction monitoring.

Next, I developed practical leadership strategies involving data quality reviews, fairness evaluation, human oversight, and continuous monitoring.

I used a hypothetical financial example to demonstrate how overall model performance can hide differences between customer groups.

Finally, I organized these concepts into a professional leadership framework and developed a reusable checklist to help teams evaluate AI risks and make informed decisions.
