About Me

I am a software engineer with experience in financial technology, including roles at JPMorgan Chase and Nomura. My work has involved large-scale applications, data pipelines, and order management systems supporting global financial exchanges.

Working with these systems has given me a strong understanding of real-time data processing, reliability, and the importance of accurate information.

I am now expanding my knowledge through graduate studies in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Indiana Wesleyan University. My goal is to combine my software engineering experience with AI and machine learning to solve practical business problems.

This portfolio showcases my AI/ML coursework, practical projects, and continued professional development.

My Value Proposition

I bring hands-on experience in financial technology, real-time data processing, and software engineering.

By developing my AI and machine learning skills, I aim to help organizations build reliable, responsible, and practical AI solutions that support better decision-making.

I believe successful AI systems require more than technical accuracy. They also need reliable data, clear communication, fairness, and human oversight.
